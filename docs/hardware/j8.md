# J8: the 30-pin link between the main board and the power board

Every one of the 30 pins gets a destination on each board, a function, a direction, a level and its evidence. The
table fills as measurements come in. It describes the original boards, and it is also the specification for a main
board of our own (roadmap stage 6). Other main-board nets are in [nets.md](nets.md).

- **Connector:** J8 on both boards, 30-way FPC, 0.5 mm pitch, flip actuator (main board: [callouts](callouts/main_component-side_micro.md)).
- **Pin 1** is the end with the printed circle on the main board; `30` is printed at the other end (`likely` from the
  silkscreen; the measurement below agrees).
- **Cable:** the cooker's own FFC, same-side contacts (type A, `likely` from the photos), so pin n meets pin n.
  For the bench: QUARKZMAN `FPC-30P 0.5MM` break-out A with a 100 mm type A FFC
  ([bench setup](bench-setup.md#step-1b-bench-supply-normal-way-of-working-afterwards)).
- **Header mapping on the bench:** break-out header pin n = J8 pin n (`likely`). The six supply pins come out on
  header pins 1-6; a reversed cable would have put them on 25-30. In the [bench photo](../../images/own/2026-11-04/01_bench_main-board_breakout-A-on-J8.jpg)
  the cable also runs straight, 30 end to 30 end. To close it: header pins 25-30 are open to JP5 pin 1.
  On break-out B, on the cooker's own FFC, the power board's supply arrives on header pins 1-6 and returns on 30, so header n = J8 n there too (`likely`).
- **Supply: 5.01 V** (`confirmed`, measured 2026-11-04): pins 1-6 to pin 30 at break-out B, on the cooker's
  own FFC, with the cooker live and the **main board unplugged**, so without load
  ([photo](../../images/own/2026-11-04/05_bench_J8-supply_5V01_on-breakout-B.jpg)). Pins 1-6 feed U17's input directly
  (continuity to C577's pad, `confirmed`), and U17 accepts at most 6 V ([nets](nets.md#supply)). Under load:
  not measured yet.
- **Isolation from mains:** the AC check from GND to mains earth isn't logged yet (bench setup step 1a).
- **Ground:** pins 10, 11, 17, 19, 22, 23 and 30 ring solid to JP5 pin 2, and JP5 pin 2 rings to U15 pin 4: **GND**
  (`confirmed`/). Seven pins, spread between the signal pins.
- **JP5** is the board's power header: pin 1 = pins 1-6 = U17's input, pin 2 = GND, no short between them
  (`confirmed`).

## What the photos suggested, and what the meter said
From the top layer of the main-board mosaic (`guess` per pin), checked against the measurements above:
- **GND candidates** before the meter: 7, 9-12, 18, 20, 22?, 24, 30 (no copper visible on the top layer). The meter found 10, 11, 22 and 30 on JP5 pin 2's net, but 7, 9, 12, 18, 20 and 24 not. Pins 17, 19 and 23, read as
  signals to vias, are on that net too: their vias drop straight to it. Pins with no visible copper and no ground (7, 9, 12,
  18, 20) may be not connected at all.
- **Signals with long top-layer traces:** 8 and 13-16, running up-left toward the chips. Traced on 2026-11-04: only 8, 13 and 14 leave as traces, and they run to U7's corner; 15 and 16 show none on the top layer.
- **Signals to vias** within 1-2 mm of the connector: 21, 25, 26, 28, 29 (and 27?).

## Open pins and suspects
Not mapped yet (2026-11-04): **17 pins on the main board**, and **all 30 on the power board**. For pins 1-6 (supply)
and the seven GND pins the connection is `confirmed`; the supply voltage isn't measured.

| Pins | What is known | Suspect (`guess`) |
|---|---|---|
| 7, 9, 12, 18, 20 | not GND; no top-layer copper | not connected, or routed under the connector body to an inner layer |
| 8, 13, 14 | not GND; three long top-layer traces that run, in a bundle, to the left-pin corner of U7 (CC2640R2F, the Bluetooth chip), past TP13/14/42/43. None of them ends on TP13 | the control lines, run by U7: heater and pump drive, or a serial link to a power-board MCU (U2 there, unidentified) |
| 15, 16 | not GND; no top-layer trace leaves the contact | routed under the connector body to an inner layer, or not connected |
| 21, 27, 28, 29 | not GND; short stubs to vias | signals that continue on an inner or the bottom layer |
| 24, 25, 26 | three separate lines, each with a cap and a resistance to GND; 25 and 26 read the same | analog inputs: the temperature sensor, with 25/26 as two identical channels |

What has to cross the cable, from the user manual in the FCC filing (exhibit 3999188): a 1200 W heater whose heating
power is set from the app, a pump whose flow rate (up to 12 L/min) is set from the app (p. 18), and a sensor in the
water path (p. 24). With the relay (`likely`) and the sensor finger on the power board ([power board](power-board.md)),
that makes at least a heater-drive line, a motor-drive line (PWM?, maybe with speed feedback) and one analog sensor.
Possibly also a thermal cut-off status and a mains zero-cross signal (`guess`). That is fewer than 17, which fits
's guess that some pins are spare or not connected.

How to close them, cheapest first:
- **Diode mode on all 17** (measurements 1-2 below). An open reading both ways means not connected. About 0.5-0.7 V
  one way means a chip pin's protection diode, so a live line.
- **Desk work:** follow 8 and 13-16 to their ends on the mosaic (`tools/trace`). The chip pin each one lands on, and
  its datasheet function, name the line.
- **The power board out of the tube** (unplugged, discharged): where each pin goes there (relay driver, motor driver,
  sensor finger) names most of them at once. Campaign step 4, no new tools needed.
- **Under power, later:** the [j8probe](../../tools/j8probe/README.md) ESP32 on each break-out, listen-only, and the
  logic analyser (due 2026-11-20 to 25) on the live link.

## Next measurements
Unpowered, from the break-out header:
Done: JP5 pin 2 to U15 pin 4, JP5 pin 1 to U17 pin 7/8, JP5 1-2 no short, pins 24-26 to 3.3 V, VIN and each
other. GND is U15 pin 4: the `XMC` SOP-8 at the board edge next to the J14 footprint; its pin 1 is the leg at the
moulded dimple, next to two white silkscreen dots, pin 4 the far end of that row, pin 8 (3.3 V) across from pin 1 at
the dimple end of the other row (pin 5 is the one diagonally opposite pin 1; SOIC-8 numbering runs counter-clockwise).
1. Pins 24-26: the unit of the settled readings, the resistance with the leads swapped (a change means a semiconductor
   in the path), and diode mode both ways to GND (about 0.5-0.7 V one way means a chip pin's protection diode).
2. Every other header pin to 3.3 V (U15 pin 8) and to VIN (JP5 pin 1), with diode mode both ways to GND. No beep to
   either supply makes "signal or not connected" measured (working assumption since 2026-11-04). Open both
   ways in diode mode means not connected on the main board. A 0.4-0.7 V drop means a chip pin.
3. Optional before power: R589 and R590 (U17's output voltage). U17 pin 1/2 to U15 pin 8 is done.

Then the supply under power: about 5 V on JP5, with the pre-power checklist in
[bench setup](bench-setup.md#step-1b-bench-supply-normal-way-of-working-afterwards).
